Classic "Holy Guacamole" recipe
This version is simple, fresh, and highlights the flavor of the avocados. [3, 6, 7]
Ingredients

• 2-3 ripe Hass avocados
• 1/2 small white or red onion, finely diced
• 1-2 jalapeño peppers, finely diced (remove seeds for less heat)
• 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped
• 1 small tomato, seeded and diced
• 1-2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
• 1/2 teaspoon kosher or sea salt, or to taste
• Freshly ground black pepper, to taste [3, 8, 9, 10, 11]

Instructions

1. Prepare the avocados: Cut the avocados in half lengthwise, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a medium-sized bowl.
2. Mash the avocados: Use a fork or a potato masher to mash the avocado to your desired consistency. For a chunky texture, mash lightly; for a smoother one, mash thoroughly.
3. Combine ingredients: Add the diced onion, jalapeño, cilantro, and tomato to the bowl. Stir to combine.
4. Season: Squeeze in the fresh lime juice and sprinkle with salt and pepper.
5. Mix and serve: Gently mix everything together. For the best flavor, serve immediately with your favorite tortilla chips. [3, 12, 13, 14, 15]

Flavor variations

• Smoky and savory: Add 5 pieces of thick-cut, cooked, and chopped bacon for extra crunch and smokiness.
• Warm and earthy: Stir in 1/4 teaspoon of ground cumin and a pinch of cayenne pepper with your seasonings.
• Creamy and mellow: For a richer, milder flavor, incorporate 1 tablespoon of sour cream per avocado.
• Tex-Mex style: Add a few dashes of your favorite hot sauce for extra kick. [1, 8, 16, 17, 18]

Pro tips

• Prevent browning: To store leftover guacamole, place it in an airtight container and press a piece of plastic wrap directly onto the surface to remove air, which causes oxidation. Placing the avocado pits in the dip can also help.
• Choose ripe avocados: A ripe avocado will have a dark, black-green skin and feel slightly soft when gently pressed. If you remove the small stem at the top, a green color underneath indicates perfect ripeness.
• Balance the flavor: Taste your guacamole before serving and adjust the seasoning as needed. You can add more lime juice for tang or a little extra salt for flavor. [8, 19, 20, 21, 22]
