

One of the big lessons learned so far in trying
to learn a more functional approach to
programming is...


I've been trying to tackle functional
programming on qnd off now for what is probably
close to 5 years more or less. It has been a
learning adventure. It has been fun and exciting
at times, and some times head scratching and
bewildering. Actually, it hasnt really been all
that bad, it's mostly a matter of patience in
finding the right solutions. In a way, functional
programming is kind of like magic, because once
you have the function, whatever it does is available
to you in a single poof.

So, yherefore, being like magic, it is going to
have its draw backs  not unlike most things if
not everything. I think the sane way to think
about FP, as i may have come to understand, is
that it is best utilizedfor certian programming
tasks  in the same way a chef might choose to
use a mixer over a press. Where as, in this
anology, the mixer is FP, and the press could be
OO.

My reasoning in stateing this, is that at first
for many good reasons, i really kinda went full
FP, trying to fit everything into an FP approach...
